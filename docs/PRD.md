# Product requirements — Pocketwise

## Users and problem
Students and people with irregular allowance/income need an understandable way to split each inflow into essential spending, discretionary spending and savings. Mobile-first and offline access are first-class requirements.

## User stories
1. As a student, I can enter an allowance and instantly see 50/30/20 allocations.
2. I can classify spending as Needs or Wants and cannot overspend the selected envelope.
3. I can bring forward leftover Needs/Wants into the next later-date allowance and have the combined amount reallocated.
4. I can see my balances, income, expenses and trends.
5. I can mark savings moved to an external bank/e-wallet without implying the app moved money.
6. I can ask the coach about my own finance records, even offline.
7. I can export/restore data and install the app on Android.

## Success criteria
Correct cent-level allocations, accurate rollover accounting, clear empty/error states, mobile layout, installable PWA, reliable local persistence, and explicit privacy/transfer disclaimers.

## Out of scope
Real money movement, banking integrations, accounts/sync, investment recommendations, automatic expense import, advanced embeddings/vector DB, multiple currencies.
